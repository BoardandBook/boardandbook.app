Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026
Last updated: October 2026

Board & Book ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Anonymous by Default

When you first install Board & Book, the app uses anonymous authentication. Firebase Authentication generates a random anonymous user ID that is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.

You can use the following without creating an account:

  • Local pass-and-play games
  • Computer (Arasan) games
  • The first book in the Learn library
  • The first 5 puzzles per theme in Train
  • The daily puzzle

In this anonymous mode, your progress is stored locally on your device and synced to Firestore under your anonymous identifier.

2. Information You Provide When You Sign In

If you sign in with Apple or with email, we collect:

  • Your email address (from Apple Sign-In or email sign-up)
  • Your display name (which you choose)
  • Your @handle, if you claim one
  • An avatar (which you select or generate)
  • Account creation timestamp and last sign-in timestamp

You can use online and social features only after signing in.

3. Online and Social Data

When you use online features, we collect:

  • Friend list, friend requests, and the players you block or mute
  • Direct messages (the content of messages between you and other users)
  • In-game chat messages during online matches
  • Online game records (moves, results, opponents, timestamps)
  • Online ratings and win or loss records

This data is stored on our servers (hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH) and is required to operate the social features. We do not read your messages except as required to operate, secure, or moderate the service.

4. Progress and Gameplay Data

We store:

  • Puzzle completion
  • Reading progress
  • Saved games
  • Adaptive difficulty rating

This data is stored locally on your device and synced to Firestore when online. It is linked to your user identifier (anonymous or signed-in).

5. Purchase Data

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase status and premium entitlement.

RevenueCat receives:

  • Your user identifier
  • Purchase status and entitlement status

We do not receive:

  • Your Apple ID
  • Payment details
  • Billing information

Founding Members

Signed-in accounts with a confirmed email that reach our servers while Founding Members is open, up to No. 1,000, receive a Founding Member number, which gives the account every book, puzzle, board, piece set and theme in Board & Book. It is not a purchase, and it is not sent to Apple or RevenueCat.

  • The number is stored with your account on our servers (hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH), in our encrypted server backups, and on your devices.
  • Other players, your friends included, are never shown it. We see it in our administration tools.
  • When you delete your account, its number is deleted with it, as described under Data Retention, and is never given to anyone else. We keep only a count of the numbers issued, which is not linked to anyone.

6. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging push token in order to deliver friend, message or game alerts. A message alert shows the sender's name and the start of the message; it is delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service. Daily puzzle and streak reminders are scheduled on your device and do not use the push token. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Firebase Analytics collects usage events such as puzzles solved, chapters read, games played, and settings changed. These events are associated with an analytics identifier that Firebase generates for your device and, if you are signed in to an account rather than playing as a guest, with your user identifier. They do not contain your name, email address, or message content. Firebase Analytics also works out a general location, such as your country or city, from your device's IP address; it does not use your device's location services.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports to help us fix bugs. Crash reports include device model, OS version, and stack traces.

Both are off until you say yes. The app asks you once whether to share usage data and crash reports, and collects neither before you answer yes. You can change your answer at any time in Profile → Help → About → Analytics. Turning it off stops both on that device immediately and deletes crash reports not yet sent.

8. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Your physical address or precise location
  • Your phone's contacts or address book, which the app never reads (your friend list in Board & Book is described above)
  • Your photos, microphone audio, or browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers

Board & Book contains no advertisements and performs no cross-app tracking.

9. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

  • Operate your account and provide app functionality
  • Sync progress across sessions and devices
  • Provide online play, friends, and messaging
  • Verify premium access
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Communicate with you about the service when you opt in

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking.

10. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

  • Local data remains on your device until deletion
  • Cloud data is retained while your account is active

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your account, gameplay, online game, friend, and message records within 30 days. Some records may be retained longer where required by law (such as purchase records for tax compliance) or in encrypted server backups, which are rotated nightly and normally deleted within 16 days. If other players blocked your account, or your account was suspended or permanently banned under the Community Guidelines, we keep those blocks and that suspension or ban after you delete the account, linked only to your account ID, so that deleting it cannot undo them. A suspension is deleted once it ends.

Deleting the app removes the data it keeps on your device, but it does not sign you out: your device keeps the sign-in, so if Board & Book is installed on it again, it opens with the same account. If you are signed in, use Sign Out in Profile → Settings → Account before you delete the app.

Analytics events are kept by Google, and purchase records by RevenueCat, under each service's own retention period.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time: turn off usage data and crash reports in Profile → Help → About → Analytics, or notifications in iOS Settings

European users: Our lawful basis for processing is contract (where data is necessary to provide the account, online play, and purchase verification you requested), consent (for analytics and crash reports, which we collect only after you say yes), and legitimate interest (for service operation). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew it. We do not rely on consent to create or operate an account. Where a user is below the age of contractual capacity in their country, we rely on legitimate interest as the basis for operating their account. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and request data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@boardandbook.app. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA or CPRA. You have the right to access, delete, correct, and obtain a portable copy of your personal information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

12. Data Security

We use TLS for data in transit and authenticated APIs for server access. Account passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and never stored in plaintext. No system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Third-Party Services

We rely on:

  • Apple (payments, Sign in with Apple, push delivery)
  • Firebase by Google (authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging)
  • RevenueCat (purchase verification and entitlement management)
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (server hosting for online play, friends, and messages)
  • DiceBear (avatar artwork from the open-source DiceBear library, drawn on our own server; nothing is sent to DiceBear)
  • Cloudflare (off-site storage of encrypted backups; backups are encrypted before they leave our server and Cloudflare cannot read them)

Each provider operates under its own privacy policies.

Links:

  • Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Hetzner: https://www.hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy

Data processed by these services may be transferred to and stored on servers in the United States and the European Union.

14. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 14, and accounts are limited to users 14 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at support@boardandbook.app and we will delete it.

If Apple marks your Apple Account as covered by a law that requires age checks (in Texas today), then on iOS 26.2 and later we ask Apple for your age range the first time you go online after opening the app. We learn only whether you are under 14, 14 to 17, or 18 or older, and we use it only to decide whether online play is open to you: if you are under 14, decline, or Apple does not answer, online features stay closed. We never store the age range, send it to our server or log it. For an app update that Apple counts as a significant change, your device keeps whether a parent was asked to approve it and what they answered, or that an adult was shown Apple's notice about it; never an age. Until the parent approves, a player who needs that approval cannot use online play, friends or messages; offline play, puzzles and books are never affected.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance.
